How it works

Offline keys, readable enforcement

Pro keys are ed25519-signed strings verified offline by the hikari-license crate. There are no network calls, no telemetry and no metering, and the key material is read from your own environment so you keep your signing infrastructure.

The check lives in source you can read, which is a deliberate choice: a key check plus commercial terms, rather than obfuscation dressed up as protection. License::dev() exists for evaluation and for the test suite.

The open core stays MIT / Apache-2.0 regardless of Pro status. Stills, text shaping and font handling are not going behind a key.

Full terms are in LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md in the repository.
