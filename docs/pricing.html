How licensing works

Pro keys are ed25519-signed strings, verified offline. No network calls, no telemetry, no metering. Enforcement is the key check plus the commercial terms — stated openly, no DRM theater. License::dev() exists for evaluation and tests.

Full terms: LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md in the repo. The open core stays MIT/Apache-2.0 regardless of Pro status.
